Upstream also has a Node build script, but its engine/API/tool parity has not been evaluated; switching runtimes to avoid compliance work is not justified.

Owner must choose LocalAgent's own license/public name/legal publisher and approve a competent redistribution review. Apache-2.0 or MIT are options, not selections. Separate-process LGPL does not by itself establish LocalAgent's own licensing requirements. Notices, source links and security-audit results never establish legal clearance.
